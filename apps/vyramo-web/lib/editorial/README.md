# Vyramo Diamond Editorial Core

Pipeline:

1. Niche Miner
2. Opportunity Scout
3. Benchmark Deconstructor
4. Hook Architect
5. Story Architecture Director
6. Content Universe Lateralization Architect
7. Binge Graph & Session Architect
8. Diamond Scriptwriter
9. Visual Narrative Director
10. Packaging & SEO Strategist
11. Monetization Policy Auditor
12. CEO / Red-Team Critic
13. Revision Loop
14. Final 95+ Gate

Core design rules:

- Benchmark structures, never copy scripts or distinctive expressions.
- One validated fact may branch into multiple independent theses only when each branch adds new evidence, stakes, causality, or utility.
- A video must fully pay off its own promise before handing off to another video.
- End-screen strategy is measured with impressions, clicks, click rate and same-channel continuation.
- CTR, retention and RPM must come from channel analytics; public market data is not a substitute.
- Subscriber count is context, not causal proof of quality.
- 95/100 is an internal quality gate, not a forecast of views, revenue or recommendation.
